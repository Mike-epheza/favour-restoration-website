Who We Are

Built on the Word,
Powered by Love

FAVOUR RESTORATION WORD OF GOD MINISTRIES is a Spirit-filled Evangelical church committed to preaching the uncompromised Gospel — and living it out in every neighbourhood, every family, and every life we touch.

"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord — plans to prosper you."

— Jeremiah 29:11
  • Word-centred preaching & discipleship
  • Passionate, Spirit-led worship
  • Community outreach & social transformation
  • Raising the next generation of leaders
Vision

Transform lives through the power of the Gospel

Mission

Reach, teach, and release every believer
